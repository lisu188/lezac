# Live Intro Recognition

This fixes scene recognition in the two physical keyboard helpers. It changes
no game code, RNG behavior, gameplay inputs, observation timeouts, original
comparison thresholds, or whole-game completion/fidelity flags.

## Failed Main CI

The Linux job in [main CI run 37368764559](https://github.com/lisu188/lezac/actions/runs/37368764559)
failed `bios_menu_input_live_xvfb` at `fresh-one-intro`. Its exact head was
`ad4751e55351998d73cd92482ad085c866b05308`; the Windows job passed.
The retained failure frame shows the complete Italian Level 1 introduction,
including its 620 white caption pixels. The previous predicate required seven
distinct background RGB colors. The frame instead contains six:

```text
(20,60,4), (20,60,8), (20,65,8), (20,65,12), (20,69,12), (20,69,16)
```

The original's seven palette entries need not be seven distinct colors. The
[original intro recovery](level_intro_exact_recovery_2026-07-19.md) documents
the generator: each channel starts in 0..19, its delta is in 0..29, and entry
`i` is `start + delta * i / 7`, converted from six-bit VGA components. Starts
`(5,15,1)` and deltas `(0,3,4)` produce the observed six-color set, with the
first two entries both `(20,60,4)`. This is a legal palette existence witness,
not reconstruction of the failed process's actual RNG seed.

The failure remains a failed CI observation. Its screenshot RGB SHA-256 is
`f8d71fb8a1fe037c95fdc6d443765a967e81e3966d947cf1c0b9c46eef09ab6d`.
The fixture records run/job/artifact identities and the unchanged executable
SHA-256 `b078c6440218a57dcb3c1720b0be8e625bfb2a29ced489eb9b32169a31f826f7`.

## Recognition Contract

`tools/intro_frame.py` is shared by both live keyboard helpers. It requires:

- a 320x200 RGB image;
- a top-band color set generated by at least one legal seven-entry palette,
  including duplicate entries and monochrome cases;
- an exact nonempty typing prefix of the original Italian Level 1 caption;
- the original one-pixel up/left blue shadow at every visible glyph;
- only background-palette pixels elsewhere in the complete frame.

The original-backed caption mask comes from
`evidence/intro_buffer_awaiting_20260930.png`, RGB SHA-256
`33993e7ce0a873860eee4db36245a60014a2bcfec473e9f7d0f80f165ea7b431`.
Its executed original observer and ungated physical-input record remain pinned;
see [the buffered-input evidence](buffered_menu_input_2026-09-30.md).
The complete mask has 620 white and 360 shadow pixels; the first glyph has 31
white pixels. A legal palette may itself include the shadow's blue, which is
allowed outside the caption only when that blue belongs to the palette.

This is intentionally scoped scene recognition, not background-pattern,
frame-timing, all-level/language, or original/C++ pixel-parity acceptance.
The existing input checks still distinguish typing, consumed skip, blocking
wait, ignored modifiers, fresh acknowledgement, held-key repeats and gameplay.
Each new live result also records the shared detector source hash.

## Regression Evidence

`intro_frame_contract` uses only the Python standard library, on Linux and
Windows. Its sealed fixture contains the original waiting intro, the failed
C++ CI intro and actual original single-/two-player gameplay frames. Pinned
gzip PPM files preserve the exact RGB bytes without introducing a Pillow
dependency for the portable contract.

The 17 tests cover all 26 caption prefixes with one through seven distinct
colors, monochrome extremes, a legal background containing the shadow color,
the palette parameter domain, illegal channel pairing, corrupt glyph/shadow,
extra nonbackground pixels, blank/short/wrong-format images, all 16 sealed
original menu frames, both actual gameplay captures, and shared helper use.
Synthetic palette/prefix probes are recognition tests, never original captures
or parity fixtures. The failed CI frame and original capture are separate
observations with different random backgrounds, not a pixel-aligned pair.

The full original campaign, Level 3 completion, later natural levels, full
actor/DAC state, authentic manual input, presentation timing and sound parity
remain open. This change does not close a broad completion item.

Local validation passed the 17-test portable contract on both Linux and Windows,
the 20-test window-acquisition contract, BIOS/keypad evidence validators,
source guardrails and the silent-launcher policy. All four normal-entry BIOS
physical-input scenarios and all three held/fresh-input scenarios passed with
dummy audio on the unchanged packaged executable pinned above. The 94 native-size
captured C++ frames were read back and matched their recorded RGB hashes; every
case exited normally with its executed harness and detector source hashes.
Four focused CTests also passed. These local results are not full CI acceptance.
