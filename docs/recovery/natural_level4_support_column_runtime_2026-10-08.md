# Natural Level 4 Support Column

## Scope

The ordinary one-player route extends the original-backed third-objective and
return-portal route from tick 11420 through tick 11740. A stationary Medium bomb
is created on the post-update boundary at tick 11466. Movement, the bomb,
support-column motion and settled rubble are observed without state injection.
The endpoint retains all three objectives, 244 destroyed structures, 22 health,
zero reserves and four Medium bombs. Level 4 requires 305 destroyed structures
and is not complete. Levels 1-3 remain the only completed campaign levels on
this route: 3/7 is campaign coverage, not a whole-game recovery percentage.

## Original Evidence

The fixture was exported from closed original-only atomic observations. It
contains 2380 presentations and 4700 mapped boundaries, including 2319 playable
Level 4 frames. The inherited first 4060 boundaries are byte-for-byte equal to
the trusted third-objective reference; 640 boundaries extend that reference.
All 7018 raw DS boundaries passed independent coherence checking, with no
normalization or substituted C++ expectation bytes.

The original raw archive has 15124 members and SHA-256
`4b480ad5e4a04cef08dad1449fd99e0b46827af23617e6b72be6e02a043b42bc`.
Independent remote readback checked member sizes/hashes, complete archive bytes
and the notes-tree anchors. The checked-in evidence manifest pins the original
producer chain, preparation receipt, coherence audit, closure identities and
retention receipts. Tests require no access to the historical scratch paths.

## Regression Contract

`natural_level4_support_column_original` executes the production ordinary-input
replay from Level 1, retaining the Level 3 completion gate, results/handoff and
the Level 4 route. It compares 2380 full RGB frames (152320000 pixels), 4700
mapped boundaries and the separately projected lifecycle, monster, marker and
terrain fields where present in the original fixture. Covered state is checked
at both present and post-update boundaries. Result-loop observations retain
their existing independently scoped checks. This is not a new full-prefix
comparison or a comparison of every actor byte.

`natural_level4_support_column_guard` rejects 27 reference/byte corruptions,
29 producer corruptions and 291 typed-state field mutations. The C++ endpoint
in `guard-input.json` is only a negative-control stimulus; all expected state
and RGB fingerprints come from the original capture. Production replay checks
also retain the shared comparator's 43 C++ projection mutations.

The fixture and evidence directories have explicit `-text` attributes. The
checkout regression checks all Level 4 evidence under `core.autocrlf=true` and
independently removes just the support-column rules to prove that only its
three pinned text files are transformed. The existing health-reward and older
evidence negative controls remain intact.

All replay children use dummy video and audio. Whole-game completeness,
original fidelity, Level 4 completion, full-prefix comparison and complete
actor/clock/sound byte parity remain false. Later campaign levels, the natural
final-boss route, two-player completeness and physical timing remain open.
